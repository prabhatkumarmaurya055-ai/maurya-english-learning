Grammar

Noun

Noun किसी व्यक्ति, स्थान, वस्तु या चीज़ का नाम होता है।

Ram is a student.
Delhi is a city.
This is a book.
My school is big.

Pronoun

Noun की जगह इस्तेमाल होने वाले शब्द Pronoun कहलाते हैं।

I am a student.
He is my brother.
She is my friend.
They are happy.

Verb

Verb काम या action बताता है।

I eat food.
I play cricket.
She reads a book.
They go to school.

Adjective

He is a good boy.
She is happy.
This is a big house.
It is a beautiful flower.

Adverb

He runs quickly.
She speaks slowly.
He works carefully.
They walked slowly.

Helping Verbs

am, is, are, was, were, has, have, had, will

I am happy.
He is reading.
They are playing.
She was tired.
We were studying.
I have a book.
He has a car.
I will go.

All 12 Tenses

1. Present Simple

रोज़ या सामान्य काम।

I play cricket.

2. Present Continuous

अभी चल रहा काम।

I am playing cricket.

3. Present Perfect

I have played cricket.

4. Present Perfect Continuous

I have been playing cricket.

5. Past Simple

I played cricket.

6. Past Continuous

I was playing cricket.

7. Past Perfect

I had played cricket.

8. Past Perfect Continuous

I had been playing cricket.

9. Future Simple

I will play cricket.

10. Future Continuous

I will be playing cricket.

11. Future Perfect

I will have played cricket.

12. Future Perfect Continuous

I will have been playing cricket.
